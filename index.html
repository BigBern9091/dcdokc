Every person charged with a crime is worthy of dignity, and a defense. The first consultation is free.

Should I answer police questions first?
No. Say out loud that you want a lawyer, then stop talking. Being polite is fine; explaining yourself can be used against you.
Is what I tell you private?
Yes. What you tell a lawyer while asking for legal advice is confidential, even if you do not hire me.
